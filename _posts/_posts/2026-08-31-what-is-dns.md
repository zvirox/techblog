DNS is one of the most important systems on the Internet.

Every time you type a domain name such as `example.com` into your browser, DNS helps your computer discover which IP address belongs to that domain.

## What is DNS?

DNS stands for **Domain Name System**.

Its primary job is simple:

> Convert human-readable domain names into IP addresses.

Humans prefer names like:

```text
example.com
